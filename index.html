Who we are

WHO
WE ARE?

DigiMeAds is a Sri Lanka–based digital agency specializing in digital marketing, website design & development, branding, and content production. We help businesses strengthen their online presence through strategic, creative, technology-driven solutions that deliver measurable results.

By combining creativity with innovation, we build engaging digital experiences that connect brands with their audiences and drive sustainable growth.

What drives us

Our Vision

To deliver measurable results through data-driven strategies, creative storytelling, and performance-led execution — giving clients a distinctive "wow" factor and a truly one-of-a-kind digital marketing experience.

Our Mission

To be one of Sri Lanka's leading digital marketing agencies, recognized for innovation, impact, and excellence in delivering strategic digital solutions.

NOTABLE PROJECT — we've completed

Colombo Kickerz Football Academy

Colombo Kickerz partnered with Abans Ceylon (PVT) LTD to host its inaugural football carnival on 22nd October 2022 — a major success with 11 football academies participating. We managed every aspect of the event's social media promotion.

The carnival has since become an annual staple, hosted consecutively in 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026.
